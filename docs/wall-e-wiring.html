Wall-E Wiring Sheet

Seven servos, four controls, one battery. Everything plugs into the Waveshare servo board with the Pico riding on top; only the controller box and the battery need soldered leads.

Before the first power-up

  1. Confirm the header labels on the silkscreen. The board's servo headers are labeled with the GP number they're wired to; GP0–GP6 is the expected order. If yours reads differently, change the pin names in code, not the wiring.
  2. Set the power jumper to the battery/VSYS position. Waveshare's note: don't select USB and battery at the same time. USB can stay plugged in for programming; it just shouldn't be the servo supply.
  3. Capacitor polarity. The stripe on the can is the negative leg. Stripe to GND, long leg to 5 V. Backwards, it pops.
  4. Pot gets 3.3 V. The ADC pins are not 5 V tolerant. Take 3V3 and GND from the Pico's broken-out pins, not from a servo header.
  5. Battery polarity. Meter the T-plug pigtail against the pack before plugging in. Red to VIN +.
  6. Find each servo's safe range before mounting. Sweep from the REPL with nothing attached, note the angles where each mechanism should stop, and clamp to those in code. A servo driven into a stop strips itself.
  7. Reset when the head moves? That's the 5 V rail sagging. The cap is the first fix; the second is not commanding both head servos and the door in the same instant.
